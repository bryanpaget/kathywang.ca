# About Me

Hi, I'm Kathy Wang, an artist and photographer passionate about exploring the intersection of traditional and digital mediums. My work focuses on capturing moments of beauty and meaning in everyday life.

## My Journey

I began my artistic journey years ago, experimenting with various forms of visual storytelling. Through photography and digital art, I aim to create pieces that resonate with viewers on a personal level.

## My Work

My portfolio includes a diverse range of subjects, from architectural studies to intimate portraits, each capturing a unique perspective on the world around us. I believe in the power of visual art to communicate emotions and stories that transcend language barriers.

## Philosophy

Art, to me, is about finding the extraordinary in the ordinary. Whether through the play of light and shadow in a photograph or the careful composition of a digital piece, I strive to create work that invites contemplation and evokes emotion.

Feel free to explore my gallery and connect with me through any of my social media channels.
